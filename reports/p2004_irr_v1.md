## Denominators and rejected items

- **not_obligation:** all 30 IRR items, each rater's yes/no. Three-rater agreement = share of the 30 items where all three gave the same answer; Fleiss' κ over the same 30.
- **Pairwise flag and class-row judgements:** only items **both** raters of the pair labelled as obligations. An item either rater rejected as `not_obligation` is left out of that pair, so n differs by pair. Class rows: the item's compared classes (4 per item), each a unit; the bootstrap resamples whole items.
- **Fleiss' κ (flag, class rows):** only items **all three** raters labelled as obligations (complete items), and all their class rows.
- **applies_to:** exact-set agreement and mean Jaccard over items both raters of the pair labelled as obligations; no κ is computed for the set-valued labels.
- Rejected items are never scored as a judgement category; the rejection itself is the not_obligation decision above, and every disagreement on it goes to adjudication.

### Settled not_obligation rule

> Treat a span as an obligation when it states a complete mandatory compliance condition that the regulated entity must ensure, even when phrased passively or as a mandatory condition on a document or process. Mark not_obligation only when the span is incomplete, purely definitional, scope-setting only, classification-only, merely illustrative or a worked example, or clearly assigns the duty to a non-regulated third party.

Evidence from the 8 disputed not_obligation calls:
- rejected `t1_md_12925::p00219`: Reject: the extracted text ends at 'develop an exit strategy that shall' and contains no complete operative requirement to annotate.
- rejected `t1_md_12941::p00192`: Reject: this is a criterion describing when an outsourced activity is critical/material, not a duty requiring an RE to perform an action.
- rejected `t1_md_12993::p00028`: Reject: the paragraph is a dated worked illustration of SMA/NPA tagging, not the general operative classification rule itself.
- rejected `t1_md_13085::p00015`: Reject: this paragraph only defines which amalgamation cases fall within the Directions; it is a scope/applicability clause, not an operative duty.
- rejected `t1_md_13127::p00541`: Reject: the USD 2 million statement is the numerical result in a worked example, not a standalone obligation imposed on the SFB.
- kept `t1_md_12983::p00018`: Keep as an obligation: the one-month age limit is an operative compliance condition the AIFI must ensure; Commercial Banks have the same rule with a six-month limit.
- kept `t1_md_13044::p00066`: Keep as an obligation: passive audit wording still creates a control the RRB must ensure; UCB has the exact valuation-at-audit rule and SFB only a broader audit-control rule.
- kept `t1_md_13108::p00309`: Keep as an obligation because the paragraph identifies 'A SFB' as the actor; PB/RCB alert rules overlap in customer communication but do not match the full deposit-proof/statement duties.

Supported by the adjudicated decisions: every rejection is an incomplete span, a classification/criterion-only clause, a worked example or a scope clause, and every overturned rejection vote was a complete mandatory condition (including passive wording). No disputed item here was rejected as purely definitional or as a third-party duty; those two clauses rest on guidelines v1 and v2.1, not on this adjudication.
