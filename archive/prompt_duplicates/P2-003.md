## P. Data Schema
**Supplementary item** (`data/supplementary/<source>/items_v1.jsonl`):
```
item_id, source: faq | enforcement | amendment | withdrawn,
source_url, retrieved_at, content_hash, published_date (nullable),
title, text, fields: {source-specific parsed fields},
cited_directions: [verbatim strings], entity_class (nullable, raw class or "unresolved"),
era: pre_consolidation | post_consolidation | unknown   (cut-off 2025-11-28)
```
**Link record** (`supp_links_v1.jsonl`): `item_id, cited_string, md_id (nullable), method: exact_ref | repeal_map | title_fuzzy | none, score (nullable), second_best_md_id, margin`.
**Failure record** (`<source>/failures_v1.jsonl`): `url, reason, http_status, retrieved_at`.

## Q. Implementation Logic
- Deterministic and seeded. Re-running `link`, `sheet` and `score` on the same inputs reproduces the files byte for byte.
- Paths are written repo-relative with `.as_posix()`.
- Text normalisation for linking reuses the candidates / matching normaliser.
- No LLM or embedding model is used anywhere in this prompt.

## R. Error Handling
- **CAPTCHA / blocked:** record it and continue. If a source's failure rate is ≥ 50% after retries, stop that source and report.
- **Session dies:** fetches resume from the per-source manifest. Only-missing behaviour never overwrites a good record.
- **A source genuinely doesn't exist** as a listing (e.g. no online "withdrawn" page): record it as not found. That is a result, not an error.

## S. Logging
Use `get_logger`. Log discovery counts, fetch successes and failures by reason, link coverage by method, and the code-zip hash.

## T. Unit Tests
- CAPTCHA and error pages are rejected, never stored.
- FAQ index → topic → Q&A split on fixtures.
- The penalty-release parser extracts quoted titles and deficiencies verbatim; near-miss titles are excluded and counted.
- Amendment parser: the amended Direction and paragraph numbers are verbatim.
- Repeal-annex extraction on a processed-paragraph fixture.
- Link order `exact_ref` → `repeal_map` → `title_fuzzy` → `none`; the fuzzy threshold comes from config; margin recorded.
- The era cut-off at 2025-11-28.
- The rule file must exist, with an earlier timestamp than the sheet, or `sheet` refuses to run.
- The sheet hides ranks and scores, shuffles candidates, and has blank label columns; the key is separate.
- `score` refuses blank labels and checks the sheet's text columns against the drawn sheet.
- Wilson intervals and decision bands at the edges (29.9%, 30%, 59.9%, 60%).
