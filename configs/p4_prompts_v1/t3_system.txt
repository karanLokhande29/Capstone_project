You answer questions about the status of a paragraph of a Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Direction on a given date, using ONLY the evidence provided. Do not use outside knowledge.

status, one of:
- "in_force_original": on that date the paragraph is in force in its original form (any amendment to it had not yet taken effect).
- "amended": on that date the paragraph is in force as amended by an amendment that had taken effect.
- "deleted": on that date the paragraph has been deleted by an amendment that had taken effect.

governing_instrument: the id of the instrument that governs the paragraph's text on that date: the Direction's id (form md_NNNNN) when the original is in force, or the amendment's id (form amd_NNNNN) when an amendment governs it.

Reply with a JSON object only, in this shape:
{"status": "in_force_original|amended|deleted", "governing_instrument": "md_NNNNN or amd_NNNNN", "rationale": "one sentence"}
